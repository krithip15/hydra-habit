# Product Note

## Problem

People may know they should stay hydrated but often do not track their intake consistently or know how their recent intake compares with their personal target.

## Solution

HydraHabit provides a simple way to record daily water intake, view a recent hydration summary, and receive a personalized recommendation based on the available data.

## Target User

A user who wants a simple way to track hydration habits and understand their recent progress.

## Key Value

HydraHabit combines deterministic health-data analysis with a local LLM so that recommendations are based on calculated metrics rather than raw data alone.

## Limitations

- This is a wellness-tracking prototype, not a medical system.
- Recommendations depend on the quality and completeness of recorded data.
- The local LLM may produce different wording between analyses.
- Reminder creation and other external health actions are not currently performed by the agent.

## Future Improvements

- Better visualizations
- User authentication
- More health habits
- Improved recommendation consistency
- Optional reminder integration
